# Scorecard and decision

Translate the headings and status labels into the user's language. Reuse candidate IDs. Keep the comparison readable without requiring a tree or another skill.

## Scope

State product, agreed outcome, target population, comparison window, source versions and material limitations. If no outcome exists, show the missing question rather than completing Strategic fit from imagination.

Put eligible complete candidates in descending order. List `incomplete` and `research-first` candidates separately with their gaps; do not place them at the bottom of the scored ranking as if their totals were zero. If Evidence = 1, leave the other scores and total uncomputed for this run.

## Rationale for each candidate

For every computed axis, include the score, one or two sentences explaining the relevant observation and rubric fit, plus its source location and quote/data point where useful. Show counterevidence and uncertainty beside the affected axis. Distinguish the source fact from the proposed score. For unknown axes, specify what is missing and how it affects the comparison.

## Recommendation and review

Recommend a focus, explain near-ties and state what evidence or score correction could change the recommendation. Ask which scores need correction and which opportunities the human chooses. The saved proposal should clearly say `awaiting human review` until a real response arrives.

After review, retain scorecard revision, the actual human response/source, approved or revised scores, selected candidate IDs, rationale and next action. Approval of the table alone does not select a candidate. An agent recommendation is not a human decision. Preserve prior revisions or summarize the change when revising an existing scorecard.
