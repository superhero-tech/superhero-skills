# Screenshot index and provenance

These are original live-site screenshots from the 2026-11-02 visual study. Open relevant images with an image-viewing tool before designing. Live browsing is optional when the requested screen can be understood from this bundle.

The images are research evidence only. They are not new-product art, a bundled illustration license, a requirement to reproduce source branding, or a complete design system. The study report's pink editorial cover is not itself a product reference.

## Limits

The study covered five Gumroad page URLs and four Work With Indies page URLs. The bundled subset includes wider and narrow frames but does not establish exact source breakpoints, full purchase/publication flows, login states, error recovery, accessibility or conversion outcomes. S13 includes only the Typeform introduction; its black lower area is observed embed rendering, not a visual recommendation. Do not invent unobserved source states when extending the direction.
