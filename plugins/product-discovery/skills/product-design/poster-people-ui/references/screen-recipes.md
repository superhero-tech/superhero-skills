# Screen recipes and review gates

Use the composition appropriate to the requested task. Recipes extend the visual direction; they are not claims that each screen was observed in either source.

## Entry / category orientation

References: S01, S05, S12.

- Give one message real scale. Put useful search, category choice or an action near it; show meaningful content early.
- Separate the illustration from the text/control zone. Use a purposeful scene, not several unrelated stickers competing with navigation.
- The source A removes its scene on narrow screens. Our adaptation can keep one compact vignette if it preserves identity and does not displace results. Recompose rather than miniaturizing the full desktop scene.
- Do not add a slogan, decorative overline, category carousel or signup block merely to fill space.

## Results / directory / saved collection

References: S02, S04, S08, S09.

- Text-heavy objects use rows with stable fact positions. A gallery remains appropriate for objects genuinely selected by visual content; the style does not forbid it.
- On desktop, a compact filter column can support comparison. On narrow screens, use a compact filter entry and suitable disclosure rather than copying the tall expanded sidebar in S09.
- Reuse result anatomy in saved lists and related results. Express the current task through useful heading/count and selected state, not another full marketing hero.
- Supply a clear result count, active filters/reset and empty-state recovery when those behaviors are in scope. Preserve real query and navigation behavior.
- Test enough varied rows to assess repetition. In a job board, approximately 20 examples can reveal oversized cards, tag noise, missing-data gaps and cumulative border weight.

## Object / role detail

References: S03, S06, S10, S11.

- Title, company/object identity and core terms form an immediate orientation group.
- Use a readable content column and a supporting facts/action panel where width permits. Keep secondary organization narrative below the role's decision-useful terms on narrow screens.
- Reuse the fact emphasis from the result card, with more room for long contract, salary and location values.
- Avoid copying product ratings, discounts or purchase urgency into another domain. Do not remove an existing legitimate domain feature merely because it was absent in the visual source.
- Validate any fixed or sticky action in the real scroll flow. Do not let it cover feedback, focus targets or the final content block.

## Organization / seller profile

References: S04, S07.

- Establish the entity's identity, essential facts and narrative; use the same result cards for its items.
- Platform people illustrations remain subordinate to employer/seller identity. Do not replace organization marks or informative photography with a mascot.
- Support a missing-logo version without leaving a large empty cover area.

## Apply / editor / form

S13 establishes only the source's third-party-form exception. The following is a proposed extension:

- Compact task header, grouped white working sections, visible labels, fine dark controls and one dominant action.
- Carry important context beside the form or in an accessible compact summary. Preserve existing ownership, authorization and validation boundaries.
- Errors explain the required repair, keep focus useful and do not rely on pink to mean failure. Success confirms the action rather than merely displaying a cheerful character.
- Place a small person illustration only when it aids orientation or a meaningful state; do not add a drawing next to every input.

## Account / settings / management

This is an extension, not directly sampled evidence. Reuse the shell, controls, section divisions, selection semantics and body typography. Keep density calm. Heavy expressive headings and characters are optional, not a requirement to decorate private working screens.

## Rendered review

Inspect a full desktop view, a narrow view, a dense repeated component and lower-page content. Use the project's required checks; do not invent a production-success claim from a static render.

Check:

- Does the UI remain recognizable without a large illustration?
- Is the primary title easy to find before supporting tags or promotions?
- Are paper and white surfaces clearly distinct, with fine consistent contours and no accidental giant radii?
- Is the primary action distinct from salary/facts and selected state?
- Are long text and missing values honest and legible?
- Do artwork crops preserve a clean text/control zone at each inspected width?
- Do keyboard focus, disclosure, validation, feedback, save/detail actions and unavailable states work where implemented?
- Do horizontal tables scroll inside their own region rather than widening the entire page?

Return concise evidence of the actual checks and identify any unverified flow, asset, font, contrast, dark-mode or deployment scope. Source observations are not a substitute for testing the delivered interface.
